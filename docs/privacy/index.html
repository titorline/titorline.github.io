Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Titorline is a local-first app: the information you log remains on your device. We don’t create accounts, we don’t collect your personal data, and we don’t use advertising tracking. The only information the app sends automatically is technical reports used to fix errors and crashes (see section 4), and those never include the content you log.

1. Data Controller

The party responsible for handling your data is Titorline. If you have any questions about this policy or about how data is handled, you can write to support@titorline.com.

2. What information the app stores

Titorline lets you log recurring activities and events (for example, dates, counters, notes, and attributes that you enter). All of that information is stored locally on your device, inside the app’s private database.

The content you log is not sent to the developer or uploaded to a Titorline server. There is no Titorline server that receives, stores, or processes your entries. Your information can leave the device only in these cases: when you generate a backup manually (see section 5) or when your operating system’s backup is enabled (see section 7).

3. What we do NOT do

  • We don’t collect your personal or contact data.
  • We don’t require registration, an account, or a login.
  • We don’t use behavioral analytics, advertising, or marketing tracking.
  • We don’t show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share the content you log with third parties.

4. Error reporting

To detect and fix errors and crashes, the app sends technical reports to a third-party error-monitoring service. We make the following choices to protect your privacy:

  • No personal data. Your IP address, network headers, and user identifiers are not attached to the reports.
  • None of your content. The reports do not include the content you log (your events, counters, notes, or attributes). They contain technical information about the failure, such as the error type, the execution stack trace, the app version, the operating-system version, the device model, and technical diagnostic logs generated by the app itself.
  • No screen recording. No form of on-screen activity recording is used.
  • Additionally, a sample of performance data (roughly 20% of sessions) is collected to diagnose performance issues.

These reports are processed on that provider’s servers, subject to its own privacy policies.

5. Backup and restore (you’re in control)

The app lets you export a backup of your data and restore it whenever you need to. In that case:

  • Data only leaves your device when you choose to export it, through the system share sheet. You choose the destination (save it to your files, send it to another app, etc.).
  • The restore process only reads the backup file that you manually select.
  • The backup file contains your information: keep it somewhere safe, since its handling is your responsibility and that of any services where you choose to store it.

6. Device permissions

Titorline only requests file-system access when you need it to export or restore a backup. It does not access your location, camera, contacts, microphone, or other sensitive device information.

7. Operating-system backup

Android offers an automatic backup feature that may include the app’s data and back it up to the Google account associated with your device. If this feature is enabled on your device, a copy of your information may be stored in Google’s cloud services, subject to Google’s privacy policies. You can enable or disable this backup from your device settings.

8. App stores

Titorline is distributed through Google Play. This platform may collect diagnostic or download data in accordance with its own privacy policies, independently of this app. We recommend reviewing Google Play’s privacy policy.

9. Children

Titorline is not specifically designed for children, but they are welcome to use it. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children: because it does not require accounts and never sends the content you log to Titorline servers, that content remains on your device (see section 4 for the limited technical reports the app does send). We recommend that children use the app with the supervision of a parent or guardian.

10. How to delete your data

Because all of the content you log is stored locally, you have full control over it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data from your device settings or by uninstalling the app. When you do, the information is permanently removed from that device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in the app or in applicable regulations. The current version will always be available on this page, with its last-updated date.

Questions? Write to us at support@titorline.com.
