The problem

Right now, all of this lives in your head.

  • ?When was the last oil change for your car?
  • ?Does my passport expire soon?
  • ?How long since the dentist?

Today it is spreadsheets, memory, and the occasional scare when something slips. Titorline replaces that scattered pile of notes with one place where it all gets recorded for you.

The payoff

Open the app. See the whole picture.

  • Last oil change: 5 months ago.
  • Your passport has expired!
  • Two years since the dentist!

One timeline, anchored on today. What just happened sits below the line, what is coming sits above it. No digging, no dashboards to build. The record surfaces the patterns on its own, including the "it has been how long since...?" ones.

What does it do?

Everything that repeats in your life, in one place.

Track the things that matter to you — a person, a pet, your car, an appliance — and assign events to them, so the app thinks the way you do.

  • Events coming up

    Your credit card's cut-off day, a passport about to expire, your car insurance policy due for renewal. Set it once and each one shows up on your timeline ahead of time, with room to pay or renew, and a place to note the new renewal details each time.

  • How long since…?

    A haircut, an oil change, a visit to your parents. There is no fixed date, so you log each one when it happens, and Titorline shows you when it last happened, with stats on every event.

  • Anniversaries, on autopilot

    Log your wedding day or the birth of your kids, and Titorline creates the anniversary or birthday that comes back every year.

  • Break a habit, with streaks

    Count the days since you quit, from smoking or drinking to any other habit. If you slip, a fresh streak begins without erasing the history of how far you had come.

  • Subscriptions, handled

    Review the active subscriptions you lose track of over time, so you can cancel them before the next charge or reactivate them later when you need them again. The history stays, and you choose whether the next charge is monthly or yearly. Plus, keep those free trials in view before they turn into an unwanted charge.

  • Nearly 200 activities, ready to use

    Start from a carefully curated catalog spanning vehicles, home, health, finances, documents, pets, subscriptions and more. Pick what already forms part of your life instead of starting from scratch.

  • The Sunday glance

    A single timeline centered on today that gathers everything you follow. Open the app and see, at a glance, what just happened and what is coming.

  • Private by design

    Your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Back it up to a file whenever you like, and restore it just as easily.

  • Personalization

    English and Spanish available, with a built-in language toggle. Personalize the typeface, theme, and accent color to your taste.

How does it work?

Less effort to log. More insight over time.
